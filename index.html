Find the cardholders who will miss next month's payment.

A decision-support tool for card issuers. It reads six months of repayment history and flags at-risk accounts a full billing cycle before the loss lands.

Assess a customer   Score a portfolio

Default rate by September repayment status

Why it matters

Credit cards are unsecured: there is no collateral to recover when an account goes bad. Most issuers wait for a 90-day delinquency before acting.

This system watches payment history, utilisation and repayment pace instead, so a limit can be cut or a repayment plan offered while the account is still recoverable.

How it was built

Binary classification on the UCI Default of Credit Card Clients data: 30,000 accounts, 23 raw features and 6 engineered ones. The classes are imbalanced (77.88% paid, 22.12% defaulted), so models are class-weighted and judged on recall and ROC-AUC rather than accuracy.

Logistic Regression, Random Forest and XGBoost, tested on a stratified 20% holdout of 6,000 accounts.

Four risk tiers, four actions

Each account gets a default probability and is placed in a tier with a recommended response.
